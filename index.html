RECORDED IN THE REAL WORLD

Nature, at its own pace.

These are field recordings, not synthesized imitations. They keep their original pitch and natural timing. Each one is a six-and-a-half-minute loop with no audible restart: its ending flows into its beginning at the same level and tone, and passing cars, engine hum and the loudest thunderclaps were left out or softened.

Each sound loads only when selected, and the finished mix plays locally from memory.

Phone browsers can interrupt audio for calls, battery restrictions, or when a tab is closed. Screen-lock behavior varies by device; test it before relying on an overnight session. Sleep timers may finish late if a browser suspends the page.
