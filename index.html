About the recordings

These are field recordings, not synthesized sound. They keep their original pitch and timing. Each one is a six-and-a-half-minute loop whose end runs into its start at the same level and tone, so you can’t hear it restart. Passing cars, engine hum and the loudest thunderclaps were left out or softened.

Each sound loads only when you turn it on, and the mix plays from your phone’s memory.

Phones can still interrupt audio for calls, low-power mode or a closed tab. Try an overnight session once before you rely on it. A sleep timer can finish a little late if the browser pauses the page.
